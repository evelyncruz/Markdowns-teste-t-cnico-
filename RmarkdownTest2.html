1. Introduction

This analysis investigates bird movement patterns in relation to time of day, flight altitude, and tidal conditions. The workflow quantifies hourly movement rates and flight altitude, compares bird activity between day and night, and evaluates the relationship between movement patterns and the timing and height of the next high tide. Descriptive statistics, non-parametric comparisons, and Spearman correlations are used to characterize temporal and environmental variation in bird movements.

2. PREPARE DATE-TIME VARIABLES

Converting date and time

bird_track$date_l <- as.POSIXct(
  bird_track$date_l)
tidepeak$data <- as.POSIXct(
  tidepeak$data)
# Selecting high tide records
tide_high <- tidepeak %>%
  filter(mare == "Preia-Mar") %>%
  arrange(data)
# Finding the next high tide
bird_tide <- bird_track %>%
  arrange(date_l)

next_index <- findInterval(
  bird_tide$date_l,
  tide_high$data) + 1

next_index[next_index > nrow(tide_high)] <- NA
bird_tide$next_high_tide <- tide_high$data[next_index]
bird_tide$next_tide_height <- tide_high$altura[next_index]
